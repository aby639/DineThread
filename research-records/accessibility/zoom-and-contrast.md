# Browser zoom on the customer menu

Chrome's zoom control showed 200% at 0:02 on 28 November 2026. Bibek Rai scrolled through the menu and opened Crispy Rosemary Potatoes. At 0:29 the dialog displayed the quantity controls, price and Add button. He used the pointer to add three portions. At 0:44 the cart showed $16.50. The order-status panel displayed the bill acknowledgement at 1:14. [Browser zoom and dish controls](retest-2026-11-28/browser-zoom.png)

The visible Add button and completed purchase support this pointer journey at 200%. A keyboard focus ring on Add and numerical contrast ratios remain unverified in this run.
